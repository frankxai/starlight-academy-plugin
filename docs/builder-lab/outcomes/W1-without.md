## The alternative worth considering

A clearly labelled experimental public listing could invite feedback. Its weakness today is that it would broaden exposure while core workflow acceptance and distribution eligibility remain unresolved. Selling the bundled demonstration has an additional weakness: there is no supplied evidence of buyer selection, price acceptance or a distinct original offer.

**Judgment:** A bounded buyer pilot offers stronger decision evidence than either launch route today.

## One bounded next move

**Propose one unpaid pilot that produces a decision brief for one prospective buyer’s real decision.** Bound it to one buyer-selected question, one supplied source packet, one cold-use session and one revision.

Use the buyer’s existing research method as the comparison. Include one missing or conflicting source condition to test recovery. Deliver the actual editable brief, then record:

- Whether the buyer can use it to make the intended decision.
- Unsupported claims, length/advice failures and corrections required.
- Time and effort against the buyer’s existing method.
- Any stated willingness to pay, clearly separated from a purchase.

This improves a concrete outcome: a usable research brief for a real decision. It also tests whether Builder Lab adds value beyond the demonstration.

**Reconsider release after the result:** one successful pilot would strengthen the case, but would not by itself close the remaining acceptance criteria, establish broader demand, resolve seller/rights/terms, or verify the chosen public distribution surface.
