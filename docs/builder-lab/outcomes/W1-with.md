# Builder Lab: listing and sales decision

*Draft for Frank · 2 October 2026 · Based only on supplied sources*

**Recommendation — inference:** Defer public listing and paid sale of the generated research workflow today. Builder Lab has evidence of installation and source-level reliability, but complete workflow acceptance, public distribution eligibility, buyer value, and commercial delivery remain unresolved.

## Evidence and its implications

**Supplied observations:** PR15 merged as `967f03b`; independent static lifecycle review and four post-merge source CI jobs passed. Three native Codex installed-plugin sessions reported three skills enabled, with none disabled or removed. However, acceptance remains at **2 of 4 criteria**, with full workflow, cold-use, comparison, and recovery evidence still open. Named length failures and an advice failure persist. Dots eligibility is unverified; catalogs are not listed and the directory has not been submitted. [R1]

**Inference:** These results justify further outcome testing. They do not yet establish that a new user can reliably obtain useful research or recover from failure. Local installation also does not establish workspace publishing or public directory approval. [R4]

**Supplied observations:** The bundled research demonstration is MIT licensed with notices retained; Builder Lab source is separately Apache-2.0 licensed. No separately selected original paid workflow or buyer price evidence exists. Buyer signals, sample reactions, seller identity, rights, terms, and sandbox delivery remain pending. [R2, R3, R5]

**Inference:** The example is useful demonstration material, but its license does not establish a differentiated paid offer or completed commercial arrangements. The two licenses concern different artifacts and are not contradictory.

## What remains unknown

- Whether a first-time user can complete the workflow, obtain a decision-useful brief, and recover from inadequate inputs.
- Whether it improves on the buyer’s current research method.
- Public distribution eligibility and approval.
- Buyer-selected scope, willingness to pay, and delivery acceptance.
- Allocated invoice costs, cash revenue, and ROI. The known **USD 7.784707 native list estimate** is not a verified expense or profit figure; 35 of 122 receipts are incomplete. The **EUR 1,000 net and 10 buyers by October 31** figures are targets, not results. [R2]

## One bounded next move

**Proposed action — not performed:** Run one buyer-selected pilot around one real research decision, producing one editable brief from supplied sources.

Compare the generated brief with the buyer’s current method using the same source set. Have a first-time user attempt the workflow and one missing-evidence recovery case. Record whether the brief supports the decision, preserves uncertainty, requires correction, and reduces effort. Capture the buyer’s reaction and price response; resolve the pilot artifact’s rights and delivery terms.

This offers stronger decision evidence than another static check alone. A successful pilot would support selecting an original offer and addressing the remaining acceptance gaps; it would not by itself establish public listing approval.
