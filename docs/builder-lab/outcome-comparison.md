## Alternative and measurement

Both arms used the same native Codex client, source packet and command boundary at accepted source `967f03be641dcfcaa1794ed81431e112be4f50ef`. The generated MIT example is version0.1.1, scaffolded by Builder Lab0.1.3; its ZIP hash is `fdc40d65c85efb69e827b25dbf0914b9fd246a4d07b8102b79564a0078a12825`. This is repository-skill discovery, separate from PR15's installed-plugin lifecycle. Without disables only this generated skill; other host descriptors remain in both arms. With reads the applicable SKILL.md once per task; without made no observed tool calls. A local discovery diagnostic found one/zero generated-skill descriptors and is not a model receipt.

With: 181,297 processed tokens and 184.838 CLI call wall-clock seconds across three calls. Without: 91,922 tokens and 180.638 seconds. The runner measures process launch through completion, including client overhead. Native cost is unavailable. Input may include context again after the skill-read turn; cached input and reasoning are counted once in the disjoint accounting buckets. Cache/order/service variation and tool turns prevent treating these totals as causal performance or cash savings.

The lead inspected all actual outputs against the case criteria, unblinded, and made no factual repairs or edits to the retained drafts. Human inspection/repair time was not measured. Human buyer usability, willingness to pay and editing cost remain unknown. One run per arm and three selected tasks do not support a general success rate or statistically established quality advantage.

## Product decision and remaining gates

The demonstration supports a consistent evidence convention. These tasks show no reason to price generic briefing above the capable baseline. The next paid hypothesis must solve a buyer-specific task using original rights-cleared inputs and demonstrate usable output, revision/export/recovery and comparative effort with the buyer's current method. No buyer selection or sale is inferred.

These cases have no hard word limit and do not replace the original five-positive/three-negative parent-host suite. P2/P4/P5/N2/N3 length and N2 advice failures remain. Missing-source draft recovery does not establish application interruption recovery or cold-use installation. BL-01 remains 2/4. Dots, catalogs, directory, seller eligibility, licensing/consumer/privacy approval, sandbox commerce and cash ROI remain separate pending gates.

The model workers completed. The owned profile is absent and parsed global configuration semantics match the pre-run state. Private fixtures and native traces remain for audit; source instructions were treated as data. Observed command compliance is not universal permission enforcement or filesystem access revocation.

This outcome packet requires independent exact-revision static review and CI before merge. Such review cannot certify unseen native requests, buyer demand or account access.
